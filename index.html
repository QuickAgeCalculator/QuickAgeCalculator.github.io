How is Age Calculated Exactly?

Calculating exact age is more intricate than simple subtraction because human calendar years vary between 365 and 366 days (leap years), and months contain anywhere from 28 to 31 days.

1. Complete Years & Months Adjustment

We first subtract the birth year from the target year. If the target month/day combination is earlier than the birth month/day in the target year, we decrement one complete year and adjust by 12 months.

2. Day Borrowing & Month Lengths

When the target day is smaller than the birth day, we borrow days from the preceding month based on that specific month's total days (e.g., 31 days for January, 28/29 days for February).

3. Leap Year & February 29th Logic

For individuals born on a leap day (February 29), in non-leap years, their official age increases on March 1st (or February 28 depending on jurisdiction standards), which our tool processes seamlessly.

How to Calculate Age Manually

Here is a step-by-step example demonstrating how manual chronological age calculation is computed:

Example Scenario:

• Date of Birth: 15 March 2000 (2000-03-15)

• Calculation Date: 22 September 2026 (2026-09-22)

Step 1 (Days): Subtract birth day from target day: 22 - 15 = 7 Days.

Step 2 (Months): Subtract birth month from target month: 9 - 3 = 6 Months.

Step 3 (Years): Subtract birth year from target year: 2026 - 2000 = 26 Years.

Result: 26 Years, 6 Months, and 7 Days.
